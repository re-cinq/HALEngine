The pier tramway was drawn up to run barrows out to the pierhead on rails, and the harbour
board turned it down over the cost of the sleepers.

## The rule

- A barrow is loaded at the shore end and never on the pierhead.
- A rail that lifts in frost is packed before the first tide.
