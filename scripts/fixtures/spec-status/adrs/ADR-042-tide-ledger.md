# ADR-042: Fixture Tide Ledger

The harbour office keeps its tide tables in a single linen-bound ledger rather than one
book per berth, so a berth master reads the same figures as the harbour master.

## Decision

- The ledger is kept behind the counter and never leaves the office.
- A berth master copies the figures out rather than borrowing the ledger.
